About Texas 6

This is what it’s all about.

Texas 6 BBQ is family owned and operated by Casey and Jammie Hall right here in Waco, cooking since 2023. We cook true Texas smoked BBQ: real wood, low and slow, with rubs and sides made without sugar.

We cater graduation parties, get-togethers, rehearsal dinners and family lunches, and we deliver.
